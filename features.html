Features

Run your whole roofing company from one screen.

Leads, satellite measuring, estimates, e-signatures, permits, production and getting paid — laid out in the order you actually work a job.

01

Leads & Customers

Every customer, every contact, one place.

  • Customer records with homeowner, adjuster and extra contacts
  • Flags a possible duplicate before you create it
  • Full activity timeline on every job
  • Search anything in the app instantly (Ctrl+K)
02

Measure from Satellite

No ladder. No per-report fees.

  • Trace the roof on satellite imagery — squares calculate themselves
  • Ridge, hip, valley, eave, rake, drip edge, step and headwall flashing
  • Pitch pulled from satellite, then confirmed by you
  • Printable measurement sketch for the job file
03

Estimates

Retail or insurance, built in minutes.

  • Saved templates plus your own product catalog
  • Insurance line items with RCV, ACV, depreciation and O&P
  • Change orders tracked against the original contract
  • Duplicate any estimate, or save it as a new template
04

E-Signatures

Homeowner signs from their phone.

  • Send one estimate, or a packet of options with job photos
  • Typed or drawn signature — time, IP and device recorded
  • Signed PDF saved to the job automatically
  • Signed estimates lock, so nobody can change them after the fact
05

Permits

Less paperwork at the permit counter.

  • Permit checklist pre-filled from the job's measurements
  • Satellite guesses flagged until someone confirms them
  • Upload a permit and AI reads the details for you
  • Final permit check before a job can close out
06

Production

Keep crews and materials moving.

  • Calendar, appointments and tasks
  • Work orders, plus material orders emailed straight to your supplier
  • Job photos organized by project, shrunk automatically on upload
  • Shareable photo gallery link for the homeowner
07

Get Paid

Invoice today. Get paid today.

  • Invoices built straight from the signed estimate and change orders
  • Deposit requests, deducted automatically on the final invoice
  • Homeowner pays online by card or bank transfer
  • Every payment tracked — paid, partial or overdue
08

Know Your Numbers

See what's actually making money.

  • Reports: pipeline, close rate, sales by rep, job mix, money owed
  • Job profit sheets with commission and custom deductions
  • Snap a receipt — AI turns it into a job expense
  • Reps see only their own jobs; owners see everything
